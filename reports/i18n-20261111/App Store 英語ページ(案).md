## 説明文

    That one card could be the King.

    Take Down is a two-player board game where you build a formation from a dealt hand of playing cards and race to capture your opponent's hidden King.

    Pick pieces from the cards you're dealt, line them up on your side, and choose one of them to be your King.
    Both Kings stay face down.
    Read your opponent's moves to find their King, and move so they can't find yours.
    Take the opponent's King and you win.

    ■ Every card moves differently
    2 moves one square orthogonally, 3 one square diagonally. 4 and 5 move up to two squares.
    6 through 9 move to set distances, and 10 jumps.
    J runs any distance orthogonally, Q any distance diagonally.
    K can move in any direction and also jump like a 10.
    If you place a K, that K is your King.

    ■ Learn as you play in Story mode
    In a land torn by war, fifteen-year-old Nino is entrusted with a mysterious seal.
    Across 3 phases and 21 episodes, each episode teaches you a new card and a new rule.
    No manual needed.
    (Story text and comics are in Japanese.)

    ■ King Powers
    As the phases progress, the card you choose as King gains a King Power.
    Move farther, pass the crown to another piece of the same number when your King falls, or take an attacker down with you.
    Use a strong power and you may give away which piece is your King. That push and pull is the heart of the game.

    ■ Formation bonuses
    Line up a straight and the turn order flips.
    Line up a flush and you see the identity of an opponent's piece.

    ■ Ways to play
    ・Story (3 phases, 21 episodes)
    ・vs CPU
    ・Friend matches in private rooms with a passcode
    ・Random matches (count toward your rating and the rankings)
    ・Nearby matches with devices around you
    ・Take Down Puzzle (a new puzzle every morning)

    ■ King skins change the board
    Set a Foil skin from the Gacha as your King, and an "Area" rises on the 9×9 board.
    Earth reveals an opponent's piece, Sea pulls enemies in, Ice freezes a piece,
    Sky transforms, and the Palace promotes. Build a strategy around each Area.

    ■ Titles and rankings
    Earn titles from matches and missions, and show them to your opponents.

    Online opponents are shown by nickname.
    You can report or block players who behave inappropriately.

    All matches are free to play. Skins come from the Gacha.
    Gacha Tickets are earned by playing, and can also be obtained with Gems (in-app purchase) or by choosing to watch an ad.
    Drop rates are shown on the Gacha screen.

## 新機能(英語の画面が入る版)

    Take Down is now available in English. Menus, rules, the tutorial, and missions are shown in English on devices set to a language other than Japanese. (Story text and comics remain in Japanese.)
